# AMUD Skills editorial v3 image sources

All bitmap assets in this folder were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool and optimized locally into AVIF and WebP device crops.

Shared direction: warm editorial photography, Moroccan red `#B84A4A`, muted gold `#D6AE4B`, charcoal `#252525`, ivory accents, authentic Moroccan people, no text or watermark.

- `hero-journey`: Moroccan student with backpack and notebooks between Moroccan and German architectural cues.
- `borderless`: young Moroccan professional with backpack against an editorial Morocco setting.
- `language`: young Moroccan learner in a cream sweater, warm red and gold shapes.
- `laptop`: AMUD Skills product interface on a laptop in a warm workspace.
- `city`: German city and river landscape for the four-step journey.
- `career-health`: two Moroccan nurses preparing care supplies.
- `career-logistics`: Moroccan warehouse professional scanning parcels.
- `career-truck`: Moroccan truck driver beside a stationary truck.
- `career-bus`: Moroccan bus driver at the wheel of a stationary bus.
- `career-mechanic`: Moroccan mechanic diagnosing a car engine.
- `career-automotive`: Moroccan automotive worker on an assembly line.
